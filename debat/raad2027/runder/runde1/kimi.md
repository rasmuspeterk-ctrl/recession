# Kimi K3 — Round 1

## A) Agenda answers

**Q1 (λ).** Keep fixed λ = 1. This is the one decision that is *not* post hoc: ablation9 was pushed before the run, fixed λ was its primary arm, and the preregistered comparison confirmed it (+27.05 vs +18.32). The econometric case is stronger than "within 2 SD": inner folds are smaller than the refit window, and optimal shrinkage grows with n, so nested CV systematically over-penalises the full-window refit — that is the −9.4 pp in 1970s–80s origins (λ = 30–100) and it is bias, not noise. Headline from 2027: the preregistered primary, fixed-λ walk-forward (expected ≈ +21 % per F35), with nested and oracle-free printed mandatorily beside it; no figure ever appears alone.

**Q2 (selection, gate).** Amend §4 formally: no per-origin selector is ever run; curve-only is operational by council lock. The selector's inner-CV advantage correlates with realised advantage at r = −0.16 and is carried by 6 rows from 1947–48 — it is a noise amplifier, and every non-degenerate variant loses 9–10 pp. Gate: two-tier, never switches anything. Δ = skill(F5) − skill(curve) on common walk-forward origins; |Δ| > 4.42 pp → council agenda; |Δ| > MDE_gate = 1.96 · SD_block(Δ), recomputed each refit by episode-block bootstrap (currently ≈ 21 pp) → stated as evidence. The gate's MDE must be printed next to the gate: a 21 pp bar with 8 onsets detects only catastrophe. Post hoc but acceptable: we follow a preregistered ablation whose prediction ("curve chosen from 2000") was *falsified* — that is the preregistration working, not us fitting results.

**Q3 (frequency).** Quarterly. The comparison is unpaired (different origin sets, different base rates); the paired evidence is the thinnings, of which phase 0 is *worse* than quarterly and the 0.02 pp failure is a knife-edge no decision should rest on. Decisive point: monthly origins overlap 11 months, quarterly 3 quarters — effective samples are ≈ 591/12 ≈ 49 vs 198/4 ≈ 50. Monthly adds no independent information; +2.19 pp < MDE 2.99 is consistent with that. Post hoc either way; status quo plus noise-level difference makes retention acceptable. Publish both readings of §2.

**Q4 (start).** Keep 1947Q2. On the 158 common origins the three starts differ by ≤ 1.4 pp — noise; the P spread (14.9/12.4/10.4) never changes the verdict. Moving now would be chosen knowing it cuts P 2.5–4.5 pp and costs up to 3 of 8 onsets — an irreversible power loss for a cosmetic gain. Acceptable despite being post hoc because we keep the locked constant, not a result-selected one. Print the three-start P line in every ledger row permanently.

**Q5 (demotion).** Documentation only. The incumbent set is an artifact of which symmetry one picks — "incumbent-as-challenger" yields curve-only, "+2/+2 on the drop effect" demotes nobody — so "5 incumbents pass" must be footnoted as rule-dependent wherever F5 is printed. Any future search (council mandate + sealed spec) uses one symmetric bar: a feature is dropped iff the drop gains ≥ +2/+2 pp, ≥ 7/8 LOEO folds at level, sign stable — the challenger bar applied to drops. E4's curve+cape (+29.69, fails G2/G3/G5, below MDE) stays dead.

**Q6 (content trigger).** Adopt from the 2027 refit. Exact rule: on each check, compute eligible_fit at current R; if it contains any positive label or relabel absent from the manifest, refit, pin, log. Cost: 18 net refits per 49 years; effect: 53/588 months changed, mean |ΔP| 0.13 pp, max 1.05 pp. Acceptable despite being post hoc because the effect is tiny and sign-neutral, and the rule removes a lag that depended on NBER's trough-announcement habits — an institutional accident, not economics.

**Q7 (ledger key).** Do not amend a single character; it was pushed before row 4, and the base-rate generosity (logged 22.8 % vs realised 15.3 %) and the censoring assumption are now known — changing either is goalpost-moving after the score. Publish a supplement with all four numbers side by side: engine censoring +24.41 %, no-USREC-vintage +28.95 %, oracle +33.28 %, ex-post pooled base rate +21.2 %, plus per-block skills and cluster-t 1.37 with block-bootstrap CI. Public statement, verbatim-worthy: "At 9 cycle blocks the ledger reaches 80 % power only after ≈ 150 years; naive tests reject a true null 36–53 % of the time at 5–20 years. The ledger is a transparency instrument; skill claims rest on the walk-forward."

**Q8 (EBP/Baa).** Close Baa-10y permanently unless redistribution rights are obtained — a public model with a local-file input is not reproducible, full stop. Defer EBP: the review has seen onset-aligned values at 6 of 8 onsets, so any spec written now is authored under contamination and its evidentiary value is permanently discounted; with MDE ≈ 4.4 pp and 3 pre-1990 onsets it is also near-powerless. Re-examine only after ≥ 12 monthly snapshots show a non-revising history, with the contamination disclosed in the sealed spec.

**Q9 (L_R).** Peak-only for the label floor: L_R = max over episodes of (peak announcement − peak) in months, floored at 18; trough announcements do not determine onset labels, and switching to all-announcements now would be chosen knowing it moves L_R to 21 and changes eligible origins — textbook post hoc. Separately, audit trough-announcement lags for the open-episode *censoring* rule, where they are the relevant quantity; that is a different parameter and must not be conflated with the floor.

**Q10 (ratification).** Ratify all four. Conditions: dual-logging of P on the published monthly figure (GS10 − TB3MS) continues until the 2027 refit settles the live-input definition; the minutes record that the live-input change and live censoring were made without mandate and are ratified ex post — the breach is noted, not blessed. The tripwire move restores an R3 decision and the log-day rule is already in the key; both trivially ratified.
