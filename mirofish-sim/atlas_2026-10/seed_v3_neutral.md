# US Macro & Market Situation Report — October 2026

This report lists observed data only. It contains no model forecasts, no warning thresholds and no
assessment of which indicators matter. All figures are the latest published values as of 27 October 2026.

## 1. Real economy

- Real GDP growth, annualized: +2.5% (Q1 2026), +2.2% (Q2 2026, revised up from a first estimate of +1.5%). The Q3 2026 estimate is due at the end of October.
- Unemployment rate: 4.2% (September 2026), 4.1% in July and August.
- Payroll employment: +0.3% year on year (September 2026). Household-survey employment: −0.3% year on year.
- Initial jobless claims: about 198,000 per week (4-week average, mid-October 2026); about 14% lower than a year earlier.
- Continuing claims: 1.73 million (early October 2026), 11% lower than a year earlier.
- Temporary-help employment: +0.6% year on year. Average weekly hours in manufacturing: 42.0.
- Industrial production: +2.3% year on year (September 2026).
- Real personal income excluding transfers: +0.8% year on year (August 2026). Real personal consumption: +2.6% year on year.
- Building permits: 1.40 million annualized (September 2026), 3% lower than a year earlier.

## 2. Prices and energy

- CPI inflation: 3.7% year on year (September 2026); annualized 4.6% over the last three months.
- PCE inflation: 3.4% year on year (August 2026); annualized 1.0% over the last three months.
- Oil: WTI $108 per barrel and Brent $115 (20 October 2026), up about 85–90% from a year earlier.
- Retail gasoline $4.28 per gallon (+40% year on year); diesel $6.28 (+71% year on year).
- Natural gas (Henry Hub): $3.10 per MMBtu (+4% year on year).
- Producer prices year on year (September 2026): fertilizer +12%, nitrogen fertilizer +21%, grains +32%, non-ferrous metals +34%.

## 3. Monetary policy and interest rates

- The Federal Reserve raised its policy rate by 0.25 percentage points in September 2026, its first move after holding since January. Effective federal funds rate: 3.88%.
- 3-month Treasury bill: 4.04%. 2-year Treasury: about 4.7%. 10-year Treasury: 5.00% (23 October 2026); it closed as high as 5.31% in early October.
- 10-year minus 3-month spread: about +1.0 percentage point. The spread was negative from late 2022 to November 2024 (lowest about −1.6 points).
- Estimated 10-year term premium (Kim-Wright, Federal Reserve Board): about 1.0 percentage point.
- 30-year fixed mortgage rate: 7.32% (22 October 2026), about 1.05 points higher than a year earlier.
- Federal funds rate minus PCE inflation: about +0.5 percentage points.

## 4. Financial markets and credit

- S&P 500: about 2.5% below its 12-month high (23 October 2026); the high was set on 6 October 2026.
- Shiller cyclically adjusted P/E (CAPE): 40.7. Earnings yield (1/CAPE) minus the 10-year Treasury yield: −2.5 percentage points.
- Margin debt at broker-dealers: +31% year on year (September 2026).
- High-yield corporate bond spread (option-adjusted): 3.38 percentage points (23 October 2026), up from about 2.6 points in late August.
- Moody's Baa corporate yield minus 10-year Treasury: 1.47 percentage points.
- Excess bond premium (Gilchrist-Zakrajsek, Federal Reserve Board): −0.39 (September 2026).
- 90-day AA financial commercial paper minus 3-month Treasury bill: close to 0 percentage points (mid-October 2026).

## 5. Households and housing

- Revolving consumer credit: $1.35 trillion (August 2026), +3.4% year on year. Credit card balances at banks: $1.11 trillion, +4.8% year on year.
- Credit card delinquency rate (30+ days): 2.85% (Q2 2026), 0.19 points lower than a year earlier. Charge-off rate: 3.82%.
- Single-family mortgage delinquency rate: 1.86% (Q2 2026).
- House prices (FHFA purchase-only index): +2.6% year on year (July 2026). Median sales price of houses sold: $410,700 (Q2 2026), −1.3% year on year.
- Supply of new houses for sale: 8.5 months (August 2026).
